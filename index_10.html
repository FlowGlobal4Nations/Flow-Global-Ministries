Our Mission

Worship that flows freely. A presence that stays.

At Flow Global Ministries, our mission is to cultivate a community where worship flows freely and passionately. We believe that as the Body of Christ engages in self-abandoned worship and fervent praise, we create an atmosphere that invites and welcomes God’s presence — and in that sacred communion, our Heavenly Father responds by releasing His divine power and grace upon us, empowering us to grow and deepen our relationship with Him.

As we worship, we are shaped and molded into our true identity as beloved sons and daughters. Our aim is to lead believers into a deeper, more intimate experience with God, where His presence becomes a constant reality in their lives. By fostering an environment of genuine worship and heartfelt praise, we seek to inspire and equip individuals to live out their faith boldly and authentically.

As we collectively pursue this vision, we believe that the power of God will be manifested in and through us, bringing healing, restoration, and renewal to our communities and beyond. Flow Global Ministries is committed to being a beacon of light, drawing people into the transformative presence of God, and empowering them to walk in their divine purpose as part of His family.

Our Founder

Meet Apostle Kris Oliver

From the streets of South Central Los Angeles to church-planting in Haiti in the aftermath of the 2010 earthquake, Apostle H.K. Oliver’s journey has been shaped by radical obedience to God’s call. A former high school dropout turned Doctor of Divinity, he founded F.L.O.W. Global Ministries and Bible Institute in Compton, California, and now oversees a growing apostolic network spanning the USA, Kenya, and Ghana. Commissioned to the office of Apostle in 2022, he leads with a simple conviction: “The key to Power is Praise, Presence, and Prayer.”

Our First Lady

Meet Lady Shannon Oliver

Affectionately known as Lady Shannon or Lady O., she was born and raised in Philadelphia, PA, and gave her life to Christ at age 11. A devoted wife to Apostle Kris Oliver and a loving mother, she brings the same excellence to ministry that has defined her 18-year career as a lead project manager for one of the world’s leading technology providers. Over the years she has served in nearly every facet of ministry — from Praise & Worship to Church Administration to teaching at The School of Prophetic Development. Known for her nurturing spirit, wise counsel, and gift for connecting with people, Lady Shannon leads with administration, teaching, and creativity, always aimed at advancing the Kingdom.
