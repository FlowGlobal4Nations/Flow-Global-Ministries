Marriage & Ministry

What Excellence at Home Taught Me About Excellence in Ministry

People are sometimes surprised to learn I spent nearly two decades as a lead project manager before ever stepping into full-time ministry. But looking back, I don’t think that season was separate from my calling — I think it was preparation for it.

Excellence isn’t about perfection. It’s about showing up prepared, following through on what you said you’d do, and caring enough about the people around you to do it well. That’s true whether you’re managing a product launch or leading a Sunday morning team meeting.

What I’ve had to unlearn is the idea that ministry runs on hustle the way a deadline-driven career can. Ministry runs on presence. You can have the most excellent plan in the room and still miss what God is doing if you’re not paying attention to the people in front of you.

So whatever season you’re in right now — boardroom or living room, spreadsheet or Scripture — know that God is not wasting it. He’s shaping you for what’s next.

Prophetic Development

Hearing God in the Ordinary Moments

When people hear I teach at The School of Prophetic Development, they sometimes picture something dramatic — visions, dreams, dramatic encounters. And yes, those happen. But most of what I’ve learned about hearing God has come in far quieter places: the car line at school pickup, folding laundry, sitting in traffic.

The prophetic isn’t a performance. It’s a relationship. God is speaking far more often than we’re listening, and the goal of prophetic development isn’t to manufacture an experience — it’s to cultivate a sensitivity to a Voice that’s already speaking.

One of the most practical things I teach our students is to keep a running list — a phrase, an image, a Scripture that drops into your spirit during an ordinary moment. Write it down before you talk yourself out of it. Some of the most accurate words I’ve ever carried started as a single sentence I almost dismissed.

If you’re waiting for a mountaintop moment to grow in this, start smaller. Start today, right where you are.

Motherhood & Faith

Raising a Family While Building the Kingdom

I get asked a lot how we “balance” ministry and family. Honestly, I’ve stopped chasing balance and started chasing faithfulness instead. Some weeks ministry takes more of us. Some weeks home does. The goal isn’t an even split — it’s making sure both are rooted in the same soil.

One of the best decisions Apostle Kris and I made early on was letting our kids see ministry as a normal, integrated part of our life rather than something that competes with them for our attention. They’ve grown up watching us pray before we plan, and that has shaped them more than any lesson I could have sat them down to teach.

It’s not always neat. There have been Sunday mornings that felt like chaos five minutes before we walked out the door. But the mess is part of the testimony — God doesn’t need a perfectly managed household to move powerfully through a family.

To every mother building something for the Kingdom while also building a home: you are not behind. You are exactly where grace has positioned you.
